What this site will not do

The boundaries are the point. They are also what keeps this site legal.

No advice, no calls

There are no buy, sell or hold recommendations anywhere on this site — not for schemes, not for stocks. LearningFundClear is not a SEBI-registered research analyst or investment adviser, and does not act as one.

No return promises

The calculators take a return you type in and show arithmetic. Nothing here forecasts what any fund will earn, and nothing should be read as a guaranteed or expected outcome.

No exchange price feeds

There are no NSE or BSE quotes, no stock screeners and no tips. Only AMFI's published mutual fund NAVs, which are free to use and carry none of the licensing problems of live exchange data.

No accounts, no tracking

No sign-up, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising. Nothing you do here is profiled or sold. See the privacy notice.

No distributor role

LearningFundClear does not sell schemes, does not earn commission, and has no affiliate arrangement with any fund house. That is why the Direct-versus-Regular comparison can be shown plainly.

Data you can check

One source, one file, regenerated by a script you can read and run. Every figure can be verified against AMFI's own file — see methodology.
