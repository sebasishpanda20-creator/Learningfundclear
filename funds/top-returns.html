What the risk label means

Historical volatility is a calculated risk proxy, not the scheme’s official SEBI Riskometer. The official Riskometer is not supplied by this feed; check the AMC factsheet before investing. Low historical movement does not establish low credit, liquidity or future market risk.

We calculate annualised volatility from 12 completed monthly NAV returns: Lower <5%, Moderate 5–<12%, High 12–<20%, Very high ≥20%. Missing history is labelled Unknown. These are portal-defined bands, not official risk classifications.

Return methodology and excluded data

1Y is absolute NAV growth; 3Y and 5Y are annualised growth over the actual elapsed dates. The anniversary observation uses the preceding available NAV within ten days. Missing history is excluded from that period’s ranking; NAV older than seven days is excluded. The fund-house name identifies the manager of the ranked scheme; it is not an AMC-wide performance rating.
