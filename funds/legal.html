1. Risk disclaimer and disclosure

LearningFundClear is a data publication. It is not an adviser. We are not registered with the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) as a Research Analyst under the SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014, nor as an Investment Adviser under the SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013. Nothing on this site is investment advice, a recommendation, an offer, or a solicitation to buy or sell any security or mutual fund scheme.

No recommendations, by design. This site does not tell you what to buy, hold or sell. It publishes mutual fund NAVs and scheme facts, and performs arithmetic on figures you enter yourself. Any decision about your money is yours, and should be made with a SEBI-registered adviser who can consider your objectives, income, tax position and existing investments.

Market risk. Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks. Read all scheme related documents carefully before investing. Please read the Scheme Information Document (SID) and Statement of Additional Information (SAI) of the scheme you are considering.

Past performance is not indicative of future results. NAV values published here are historical records. A scheme that has risen may fall. Nothing on this site should be read as a promise, assurance, indication or expectation of any return.

Calculators are arithmetic, not forecasts. The SIP, step-up, lumpsum and expense-ratio calculators apply simple mathematical formulas to numbers you supply, including any assumed rate of return. An assumed rate is an input for illustration, not our prediction. Real outcomes will differ, can be negative, and depend on factors outside any calculation.

Data accuracy. Scheme data is sourced from AMFI's publicly published NAV file and is reproduced with only mechanical curation. We do not correct, interpret or audit it. Figures may be delayed, incomplete or wrong, including errors originating at the source. Verify anything important against the publisher's own file, the fund house or the scheme documents before acting on it.

No affiliation, no commission. LearningFundClear is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting for AMFI, any stock exchange, any asset management company, any registrar, or any distributor. We do not sell mutual fund schemes and do not receive commission, trail income, or any other payment from a fund house or distributor. There are no affiliate links on this site.

No exchange price data. This site carries no NSE or BSE quotes, no live stock prices, no intraday data and no grey-market IPO premium. Exchange market data is licensed and redistributing it without a licence is not permitted, so it is absent by design rather than by oversight.

Limitation of liability. To the extent permitted by law, LearningFundClear accepts no liability for any loss or damage arising from use of this site or reliance on its content. See section 3 for the full terms.

2. Privacy notice

Issued under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). Written plainly on purpose.

What this site collects

Account and research data. Sign-in credentials are sent to the existing Supabase authentication service. Account profiles, journal entries, watchlists, signals, and scan-list changes use the portal’s existing database. Session tokens are stored in this browser and removed on sign-out. Paper trades are stored on this browser for the signed-in account. Fund searches and calculator arithmetic run locally. This portal includes no advertising or analytics scripts.

What the hosting provider processes

This is a static site served by a hosting provider. As with any web request, the provider's servers receive your IP address, the page requested, the time, and standard request headers such as your browser's user agent, and may retain those in ordinary server logs for security and abuse prevention. LearningFundClear does not build profiles from those logs, does not combine them with any other information, and does not sell or share them. If the site is later served through a content delivery network, the same applies to that provider.

No children's data

This site is intended for adults. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly process the personal data of a child. Do not submit personal data of anyone under 18.

Your rights as a Data Principal

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to: access a summary of personal data processed about you and the identities of the parties with whom it has been shared; request correction, completion or updating of your personal data; request erasure of personal data no longer needed for the purpose it was collected for; nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity; and have readily available means of grievance redressal. Account profiles and research records can be linked to your account. Contact the portal administrator about those records.

Consent and withdrawal

Contact the portal administrator about account access and data requests. Sign-out removes the current browser session; it does not delete server-side research records or local paper trades.

Breach notification

If a personal data breach affecting you were ever to occur, we would notify you and the Data Protection Board of India as required by the Act.

Changes to this notice

Any change will be published on this page with an updated review date. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the revised notice.
