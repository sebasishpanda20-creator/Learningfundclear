Calculators

LearningFundClear does not forecast returns. You supply the annual return you want to test, and these tools show what the arithmetic works out to — including the part people usually miss, which is what a higher expense ratio does to the same portfolio over decades.

None of this is a promise. Assumed returns are only a way to see how the maths behaves. Actual fund returns vary, can be negative, and past performance does not indicate future results. The formulas are printed on the methodology page so you can check them.

What a higher expense ratio costs

The same portfolio, held two ways. Both plans invest alike; one simply pays more out of the same returns. Set the expense ratios yourself — LearningFundClear does not publish per-scheme TER.

What is excluded. Exit loads, stamp duty, capital gains tax, and any change in expense ratio over time. A real outcome will differ. Treat these tools as a way to understand the mechanics, then get advice from someone registered to give it.
