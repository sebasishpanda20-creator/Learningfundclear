Rules and data requirements

Buy rule: completed 15-minute close breaks the previous five candles in the EMA20/EMA50 trend direction. Near-ATM contract within 2% of spot, OI and volume at least 1,000 contracts each, bid/ask spread at most 2%, quotes at most 90 seconds old. Default research levels are a 25% premium stop and 50% premium target. Exit rules use your tracked stop/target and a fresh bid. No writing of options and no orders are placed.

Current connection limitations: the index relay supplies OI/LTP but lacks bid/ask and per-quote timestamps. Individual-stock option chains, current F&O eligibility and liquidity are not connected. Those signals remain Wait; these gates are not replaced by guessed prices or OI direction.
