Which direction is the market moving?

Direction uses completed Delta perpetual candles with the selected 15m/1h or 5m/15m EMA trend, RSI(14), MACD(12,26,9), ADX(14), optional volume expansion and SMC breaks/sweeps. The 70-point evidence threshold requires momentum plus either SMC confirmation or combined RSI + MACD + ADX agreement. SMC is no longer mandatory. Points are heuristic evidence, not a probability or validated win rate. Missing volume is N/A, never invented.

Why WAIT / NO TRADE?

Every asset is checked for debit spreads, credit spreads, iron condors and long straddles/strangles. Market direction, signal confirmation, live contract prices, expiry, liquidity and risk sizing are separate gates. A neutral structure can produce a volatility watch, but without a verified volatility edge it is not labelled an executable trade.

Credit spreads & iron condors — risk-gated research

Screen all quoted legs with liquidity, completed-candle direction, theoretical defined risk, costs and capital-based contract limits. These are WATCH ONLY until verified account margin, exchange execution sequence and implied-volatility evidence are available. Never treat theoretical payoff as a fill or margin guarantee.

Historical signal backtest (underlying proxy)

Quick proxy uses historical 15m/hourly perpetual prices; validation compares the current multi-factor rule against EMA+SMC and EMA-only baselines using chronological 70% training / 30% untouched testing, trailing trend/volatility regimes, 20-bps costs and 40-bps stress. Insufficient samples are reported clearly. Neither test simulates historical options, order-book fills, actual margin or future profitability.

How the automation decides

Scan → require aligned 15m/1h trend plus weighted momentum and SMC/ADX evidence (SMC optional) → check option-chain timestamps, expiry, spread, open interest and exact contract value → compare debit spreads, credit structures and long-volatility research → size from at most 2% of capital at maximum theoretical spread loss → lock one plan for a selected 2-, 4- or 8-hour research exit watch. If no plan qualifies, do not force a trade. Credit spreads and iron condors remain WATCH ONLY because their live margin and fill risks are not broker verified.

Spread entry = buy-leg ask minus sell-leg bid. Spread exit = buy-leg bid minus sell-leg ask. Stop and target displayed here apply to the combined spread; they are not Delta terminal's independent leg order prices. Currency conversion, fees and taxes are estimates; costs, margin, executable fills and trading permissions must be verified on your broker.
