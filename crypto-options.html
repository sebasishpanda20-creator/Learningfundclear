How the automation decides

Scan → determine direction → require completed BOS or sweep → check option-chain timestamps, expiry, spread, open interest and exact contract value → compare debit spreads, credit structures and long-volatility research → size from at most 2% of capital at maximum theoretical spread loss → lock one plan for a selected 2-, 4- or 8-hour research exit watch. If no plan qualifies, do not force a trade. Credit spreads and iron condors remain WATCH ONLY because their live margin and fill risks are not broker verified.

Spread entry = buy-leg ask minus sell-leg bid. Spread exit = buy-leg bid minus sell-leg ask. Stop and target displayed here apply to the combined spread; they are not Delta terminal's independent leg order prices. Currency conversion, fees and taxes are estimates; costs, margin, executable fills and trading permissions must be verified on your broker.

Research only: no positions are imported; no live order, stop or target is placed. Options are high risk. A stop can gap past the alert and a defined-risk spread may incur additional fees and execution risks.
