How to read Buy / Sell / Wait

Buy setup means a confirmed bullish index setup. Sell setup means a confirmed bearish index setup, not an instruction to sell an existing holding or write an option. Wait means no qualifying setup or insufficient fresh data. Choose any actual contract separately; these levels are not option premiums.

How the scanner works

Trend uses the 10 / 20 / 50 EMA stack. Demand and supply use confirmed pivots with three candles on each side; zones break when a candle closes through them. Setups require a trend-aligned close beyond the previous 20-candle range, or a rejection from an active zone.

Entry is the completed candle close. The stop is beyond that candle’s extreme and at least one 14-candle average true range away. The target is twice the initial risk. All levels are index points, not option premiums or executable quotes. This page does not place trades or choose strikes.

At least 100 completed candles are required. Missing, invalid, stale or gapped data suppresses setups. Intraday setups are suppressed outside regular weekday hours; exchange holidays are handled conservatively through freshness checks. Swing history older than four calendar days is suppressed. Rules are research heuristics, not a validated return or win-rate claim.
