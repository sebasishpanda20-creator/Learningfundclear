How to read this data

Intraday prices are completed 15-minute candle closes, not live quotes. OHLC and volume refer to that candle. EOD OHLC and volume cover the full session. A published index list may temporarily contain more than 500 entries after corporate actions; we show every published member and never replace missing prices with invented values.

Use these snapshots for research. They do not supply option-chain bid/ask prices or activate live option entry and exit signals.
