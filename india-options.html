How strategies are selected

Both 5m and 15m EMA20/50 trends must agree. A completed 5m candle must break the preceding five candles’ high (bullish) or low (bearish). Bullish candidates use calls; bearish candidates use puts. Mixed conditions wait.

Automatic selection prefers a matched-expiry debit spread when the farther strike funds at least 30% of the bought premium and targets fit within the strike width. Otherwise it evaluates a bought option. This is a transparent heuristic, not an optimized or proven strategy.

Research exits: stop at 25% below entry premium; T1 at 1.5 times that risk above entry; T2 at 2.5 times. These are option or net-spread premium levels, not index targets. T1 and T2 each assume a full exit, not two cumulative profits. Stops may slip.

One-lot affordability, quoted depth, liquidity and estimated costs constrain quantity. Current exchange lot sizes must come from the connected symbol master. Sample lots shown here are deliberately illustrative.

Costs, expiry and FYERS readiness

Practice costs assume ₹20 per executed order; 0.15% STT on sold premium; 0.003% stamp duty on bought premium; SEBI turnover fees; 18% GST on estimated taxable fees; a conservative 0.04% exchange/clearing allowance plus IPFT allowance; and 0.2% slippage per side. Rates and rounding need reconciliation with your actual FYERS account before live use.

Spread exit fees hold the sold leg’s premium constant to construct a hypothetical closing pair. Actual leg premiums and costs differ. The premium debit is the modeled maximum expiry loss before costs, provided both legs remain intact.

Planned intraday rules for the future live adapter: verified NSE trading calendar, no new entries after 14:45 IST, exit review by 15:10 IST. Stock options require an event check and exclude the last seven calendar days before expiry as a conservative research rule. Stock options can settle physically; this desk does not manage delivery.

Connection stage: server-side FYERS OAuth, symbol master with current lots/ticks/expiries, completed candle history, timestamped bid/ask and depth, exchange calendar and account-specific cost validation. No live mode is enabled until these checks pass. Keep API secrets out of the browser.
