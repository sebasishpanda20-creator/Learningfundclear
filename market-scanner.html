How qualification works

Intraday: stocks 15-minute + daily trend; crypto 15-minute + hourly trend. Swing: stocks daily + weekly trend; crypto hourly + daily trend. Both timeframes need at least 100 completed candles. EMA20/50 trend agreement, a 20-candle breakout or EMA20 pullback, relative volume, turnover, and net reward/risk must all pass. Extended entries above 2.5 ATR from EMA20 are rejected.

Stops use the signal candle extreme or one ATR, whichever is farther. Targets start at 3× price risk; pullback targets are capped at the prior 20-candle extreme. Estimated round-trip costs reduce reward and increase risk. Levels are historical research references, not current executable quotes.

Score: trend agreement 30 + confirmation 25 + volume up to 20 + net reward/risk up to 25. This score is a sorting rule, not a win probability. Volume compares equal-length bars, not the same time of day. Intraday opening-volume effects can distort it.

Bearish / avoid is a direction warning, not a spot short-sale order. No orders are placed. Public data may be delayed or unavailable. Stale or missing data blocks qualification; refresh before use. No exchange holiday calendar, earnings/event filter, bid/ask validation or performance backtest is included. FYERS is not connected.
