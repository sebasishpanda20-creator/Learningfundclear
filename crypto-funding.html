Why the desk does not automatically execute funding trades

Positive funding can pay shorts, but a hedged position needs a matching deliverable spot asset, current bid/ask for both legs, margin and borrow availability, fees and taxes, next settlement time, funding-rate units and basis risk. Negative funding reverses the hedge and typically needs borrowable spot inventory. Neither is guaranteed; financing and liquidation can exceed funding collected.

A watchlist row is not a trade recommendation. There is deliberately no executable hedged order or net carry forecast while these inputs are unverified. Tokenized gold (XAUT and PAXG) is not physically delivered XAUUSD. At most 20% of entered capital is used for an indicative perpetual notional size; this is not a margin or leverage recommendation.
