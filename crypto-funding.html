3. How to use this page

Enter your INR capital. The system automatically limits the example notional to 20% of capital and calculates indicative integer contracts. Compare the possible funding receipt with an illustrative 0.318% round-trip one-leg fee, GST and slippage allowance (0.10% taker fees + GST + 0.20% slippage). This excludes the spot hedge, borrowing, price movements and taxes. We recommend waiting unless the exchange's next funding timestamp, actual fees and a matching hedge are verified.

Delta India generally settles perpetual funding around 5:30 AM, 1:30 PM and 9:30 PM IST for 8-hour contracts; some contracts have different intervals. The live countdown is shown only when the exchange confirms a contract-specific funding time through its public funding-rate stream. Entering seconds before settlement does not lock in profits, and you may not qualify for a funding payment. Rates can change, price movement and liquidations can overwhelm funding, and last-second executions can fail.

Research only; no spot hedge, funding snapshot or live orders are placed. Amounts are approximate and not actual Delta INR settlement calculations.
