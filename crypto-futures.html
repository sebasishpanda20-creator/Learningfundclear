How the scanner works and what remains unverified

Standard mode uses completed 15-minute/hourly candles; short-session mode uses completed 5-minute/15-minute candles with 30- or 60-minute planned exits. Both use the existing multi-factor trend engine. A mixed trend produces no directional candidate. Stops use the wider of 1.5× ATR(14), the structure invalidation and twice the quoted spread; the gross target is twice the stop distance. A separate EMA(20) high/low-band comparison reports a fresh closed-candle cross, inspired by the Mirrorpip sample. It is not an additional validated entry system. These are unoptimized research rules.

Entries use ask for longs and bid for shorts. Directional costs use a fresh funding stream when available, otherwise the recent REST ticker rate with a valid product interval, explicitly labeled as an estimate. No settlement eligibility is inferred from REST. Costs reserve two taker fees plus 18% GST, 0.1% slippage per side and absolute current funding over the session plus one extra interval. Funding credits are never counted as income. FX and costs are indicative, not a tax statement.

Quotes must be under 60 seconds old, spreads no wider than 0.15%, open interest at least $10,000 and 24-hour turnover at least $50,000. Top-of-book quotes do not prove that the full proposed size can fill. Stop and target are price levels, not orders.

Leverage is capped at floor(100 ÷ published initial-margin percent) per contract, up to your selected maximum. Model margin is notional ÷ applied leverage, with 80% of capital reserved as a margin ceiling; the separate 1% modeled stop-risk limit still controls quantity. Actual account buying power, liquidation price, broker margin tiers, stock-market holidays, earnings and underlying-market closures are not verified. For stock tokens, check the underlying market's current session and events before acting. A research candidate is not an execution recommendation.

Funding mode evaluates the same shortlist using a recent exchange funding stream and a 20% illustrative notional budget. It ranks a single possible receipt less one-leg costs, not net trading profit. A matching spot hedge and borrowing (for negative funding) are not verified. It never promotes an unhedged funding position to a trade candidate.

Refresh manually for a new snapshot. Changing capital or filters invalidates the previous results. This page does not track an actual position or send alerts.
